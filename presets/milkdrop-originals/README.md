# MilkDrop originals

21 original presets selected from [Ryan Geiss's personal favorites](https://www.geisswerks.com/).
His January 2021 collection incorporates favorites from NestDrop's Cream of the Crop.
This is a curated selection, not a popularity ranking.

`manifest.json` records the archive URL, archive hash and exact per-preset hashes.
The files retain their original names, author credits, equations and shaders.
`playlist.txt` defines manual browsing order; automatic playback uses a shuffled bag.

Playback uses bundled projectM 4.1.7 with unchanged stereo PCM, authored timing,
original colors and geometry. Both feedback engines continue through five-second
dissolves, with 1.5 seconds of incoming warmup. Dwell is normally 24 to 36 seconds.
A new song's cover holds for 3.5 seconds from display, then dissolves for 1.75 seconds.

Textures come from the official projectM MilkDrop texture pack in `../textures/`.
The collection engine can be rebuilt with `bin/build-milkdrop-runtime`; the system library is untouched.

The preset authors retain their credits and any applicable rights. This local
collection does not assert a new license for their artwork. Before distributing
assets publicly, resolve asset licensing and preserve author attribution.
