# Kanagawa

A calm paper-print sunset: Fuji and a low red sun above a living indigo sea.
The six-stop sky, sunset disc, snow cap and printed water come from the Journey
prototype. The left third and foreground remain open for a wave and small boats.

The sky uses `gradient-sky-v1`, with a constant 0.72 paper wash. The existing Disc
uses independent sunset paints and soft bass rings; Mountain uses its original
snow cap at (1086, 476), base 616 and width 150. `water-surface-v1` uses six depth
rows, 40 fine texture rows and 90 localized reflection marks. Bands 5 through 0
brighten the ripples and move the surface with causal flow; kick adds a gentle
reflection shimmer. Geometry stays live, including during quiet passages.

Palette: Kanagawa paper `#dcd7ba`, violet `#957fb8`, wave ink `#223249`, gold
`#e6c384` and red `#c34043`. The disc's paper-tinted reds reproduce the prototype's
faded sun; the sea retains its derived Prussian-blue paints. The finish uses the
prototype's paper fibre, grain 0.008, bloom 0.28 and vignette 0.25.

The stable composition removes Journey's chapter grades, sun travel and story
beats. It uses fewer water rows and crest accents. Wave, boat, crew and bird art
are not part of this landscape yet. Artwork and kit code use the repo's MIT license.
