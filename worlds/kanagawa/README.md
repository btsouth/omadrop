# Kanagawa

A sunset Hokusai paper print with a standing indigo wave, Fuji, two rowing boats
and recurring wildlife. The wave rolls low through quiet music, towers and
curls during loud passages, and flicks its cream claws and spray on hits. Its
base height is 340 design pixels with 650 pixels of additional rise. It never
crashes. The sea, foam and boats share the same bass-responsive swell field.

Sustained loud bass opens an eleven-second surge: the crest throws a spray fan,
gulls scatter, boats dig in, a gust crosses the sky and Fuji throws a few embers.
A separate seeded cooldown lasts 45 to 90 seconds. Quiet music cannot start it.
Fuji's thin smoke plume drifts continuously, thickens with bass and has a small
beat-pulsed vent.

The launch seed varies gull passes and a schedule of visible moments every five
to ten seconds: leaping fish, cranes, a fishing boat, a lantern boat, water gusts,
a horizon squall, a snow glint and a shooting star. A friendly Ryujin breaches
between the boats after three to five minutes, then returns every four to six
minutes. Music can advance a pending visit by up to four seconds. Each visit
submerges completely; there is no disaster or impact state.

Placement, palette, surface references and piece parameters live in scene.json.
The new ink pieces use generic profiles in the library: bird-flock-v1,
smoke-plume-v1, leaping-fish-v1, sea-creature-v1 and print-moments-v1.
The sky, sun and snow silhouette retain the earlier paper-print composition.
The original Osaka world and its authored render pieces are preserved.
